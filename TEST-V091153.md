# v0.9.1.15.3 — Song Review test cases

The Node replay `tools/offline_v091153_song_fallback.js` checks:

- A MAX / The Other Side source with complete metadata and a useless raw
  recording candidate is auto-filed **only after** its details are ready.
- During the operation the card accurately states that source metadata is
  being preserved, not that a MusicBrainz recording is verified.
- The saved entry keeps the Takeout artist, song, album, and original URL.
- The next song with missing album and YouTube link displays a clear prompt.
- Its form starts with existing source artist/title filled in, accepts manual
  completion, saves locally, and advances to the next song.
- A valid recording candidate named like the source song stays visible even
  when the artist name differs from the recording title.
- No Lidarr album staging or download is triggered by either fallback.
